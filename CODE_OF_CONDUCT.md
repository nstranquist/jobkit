# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want JobKit to be a safe and useful project for everyone. We welcome people
of every background, identity, experience level, and ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and clear.
- Give useful feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept reasonable correction and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Protect private information and report security problems privately.
- Help keep project spaces focused, constructive, and welcoming.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, spam, or repeated bad-faith participation.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This policy applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, project
discussions, and other spaces where a person represents JobKit.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact link on the maintainer's
GitHub profile. Do not put sensitive details in a public issue. For a security
problem, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer will review reports as soon as practical and will keep details
private when possible. Anyone who reviews a report must disclose a conflict of
interest and step away when needed.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, issue a
warning, limit participation, or permanently ban a participant. The response
will depend on the severity and pattern of the behavior.
